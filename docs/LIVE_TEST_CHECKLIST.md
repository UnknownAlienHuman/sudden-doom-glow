# Live test checklist

1. Record `/sdglow status` and client build/interface.
2. Test one and two Sudden Doom stacks.
3. Consume with Death Coil, Epidemic, and every active replacement.
4. Test proc expiry without consumption.
5. Repeat in open world, encounter, Mythic+, arena, and battleground restrictions.
6. Change talents/spec, page/state-driver, and Cooldown Viewer layout while outside and inside combat.
7. Repeat with Blizzard bars and the active third-party bar implementation.
8. Record every payload spell ID; do not change defaults from an unrecorded observation.
9. Check blocked-action, taint, forbidden-object, and Lua errors.
10. Append evidence to `VALIDATION.md` rather than replacing older build results.
